How to start

  • Click the toolbar button, or press Alt+Shift+R. Press it again to pause or resume.
  • Select text first to read only that, or right-click it and choose Read selection aloud (Alt+Shift+S).
  • Fast read (bold word starts): Alt+Shift+B. Speed read (one word at a time): Alt+Shift+V. Both are on the player and the right-click menu too.
  • Change the shortcuts at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.
